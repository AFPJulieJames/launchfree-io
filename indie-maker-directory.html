What is an indie maker directory?

An indie maker directory is a curated space where independent creators, solo founders, side hustlers, and small teams, can list and discover products built outside the traditional venture-backed startup ecosystem. These are the tools, apps, newsletters, and services built by people who are solving real problems, often in their spare time, with limited budgets and no institutional backing.

LaunchFree.io — The Runway, is an indie maker directory with one core promise: free to list, forever. Every legal product from every indie maker gets a fair shot at being discovered, regardless of budget or audience size.

Why indie makers need their own directory

The major launch platforms were built for a certain kind of startup, well-funded, well-networked, and ready to compete for attention in a crowded feed. Indie makers often get lost in that environment. Their products are smaller, their networks are tighter, and their budgets don't stretch to paid placements.

An indie maker directory built specifically for that audience means the products you discover are products built by people like you, solo founders solving real problems, side hustlers monetizing skills, small teams building tools they needed themselves.

Frequently asked questions

Is LaunchFree.io only for indie makers?

LaunchFree.io is built for bootstrap founders, indie makers, and side hustlers, but any legal product is welcome. We just don't favor funded startups over solo builders the way some platforms do.

Do I need a finished product to list?

No. Pre-launch, waitlist, beta, and early access products are all welcome. Many indie makers use LaunchFree.io to validate ideas and build early audiences before their official launch.

How do I get my product discovered on LaunchFree.io?

Submit your product, fill in a detailed description, and share your listing with your existing audience. Community upvotes drive visibility. Top-voted products get featured in The Runway Digest newsletter.
