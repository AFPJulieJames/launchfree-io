The bootstrap founder launch strategy

The best launch strategy for a bootstrap founder doesn't require a big budget. It requires being everywhere your potential users are, consistently, over time. Here's the framework we recommend:

Start by listing on every free directory you can find, LaunchFree.io, BetaList, Indie Hackers, ProductHunt. Each listing is a permanent backlink and a potential discovery point. Then build in public on X and in communities like r/SideProject. Share your progress, your failures, and your wins. Authenticity travels further than advertising on a bootstrap budget.

Then focus on your newsletter. An email list of 500 genuinely interested people is worth more than 50,000 passive social media followers. LaunchFree.io's Runway Digest is a free way to get in front of other founders who subscribe specifically to discover new products.
