About This Launch

FareFinderAI is the smarter way to search for flights.

We surface hidden city fares, mistake fares, cashback flights, and flexible routes. The tools airlines do not want you to know about. Over 5,000 travelers are already flying smarter.

Stop overpaying for flights. FareFinderAI finds the deals before they disappear.

Why I Built This

I was booking a flight and could not believe how much prices varied depending on how I searched. Airlines make fare pricing deliberately confusing. I built FareFinderAI to cut through the noise and surface the deals that actually exist.
