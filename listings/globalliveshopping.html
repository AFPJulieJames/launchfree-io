About This Launch

Global Live Shopping is the live commerce industry hub covering brands, creators, and platforms shaping the future of shopping.

Tracking live shopping trends, brand programs, creator opportunities, and platform news across TikTok Shop, Amazon Live, and the global live shopping economy.

If live commerce is your industry, this is your home base for what's happening and what's next.

Why I Built This

I was consulting for brands trying to break into live commerce and found there was no single place tracking this space. Every piece of news was scattered. Every creator opportunity was buried. I built Global Live Shopping to fix that for everyone in the industry.
