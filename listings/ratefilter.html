About This Launch

RateFilter is not a conventional star-average review site. You define Red Lines, such as no smoking, no pests, no food poisoning, or child-friendly, and RateFilter analyzes available reviews for evidence. Each business is scored PASS, WARNING, or FAIL against your standards. Built for local search, reviews, and consumers.

Why I Built This

Star averages hide the things that actually matter to you. RateFilter lets you set personal Red Lines and shows which nearby businesses meet them, with evidence instead of a blended score.
