About Lumora Storybooks
Lumora turns one photo of your child into a personalized illustrated storybook where they're the hero, painted to look just like them on every page. Pick a theme, add their name, and get a complete picture book in English or Chinese — first story free, no card. Read online, download a PDF, or order a hardcover.
Why I built this
Most "personalized" children's books just swap in a name over the same generic illustration — the child never actually looks like the child. That gap is why we built Lumora. Upload one photo, and the story is painted so your kid genuinely looks like themselves as the hero, on every page, across a full illustrated book — not a template with a name stuck on the cover. The first story is free with no credit card, because we'd rather a parent judge the actual finished book than a landing page promise.
