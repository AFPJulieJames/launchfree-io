About AlphaVue
AlphaVue is a multi-agent AI stock analysis and investment research platform for public-market investors. More than 20 specialized agents analyze fundamentals, market data, earnings, news, sentiment, and risk to produce evidence-backed BUY, HOLD, or SELL research views with confidence levels, bull-versus-bear debate, counterarguments, risk flags, and a traceable evidence trail. Investors can monitor stocks and follow changes to an investment thesis across global markets. A free plan is available without a credit card. AlphaVue provides research support and does not constitute personalized investment advice.
Why I built this
Public-market investors often have to assemble fragmented fundamentals, earnings, news, sentiment, and risk data across many sources. AlphaVue was built to coordinate specialized AI research agents and surface both supporting and opposing evidence in one traceable workflow, helping investors research faster without presenting personalized financial advice.
