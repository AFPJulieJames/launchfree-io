GetQRcard

Free digital business cards with permanent QR codes — no app required.

SaaSLiveFreeListed August 7, 2026
Visit site ↗

About GetQRcard

GetQRcard lets anyone create a free, mobile-friendly digital business card in minutes and share it through one permanent QR code. Contact details can be updated anytime without reprinting, and recipients can save them on iPhone or Android without installing an app. Personal use is free for life; organizations only pay for bulk creation and centralized management. Available in English, French, Dutch, Spanish, and Chinese.

Why I built this

I built GetQRcard because this was a service I personally needed and wanted to make genuinely free for individuals. Traditional business cards become outdated whenever contact details change, so I wanted one permanent QR code linked to an always-current contact card. The business model is intentionally simple: personal cards stay free, while organizations pay only when they need bulk creation and centralized management.
