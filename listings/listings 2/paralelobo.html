About This Launch

ParaleloBO tracks Bolivia's parallel (blue) and official USD–BOB rates with a live converter, exchange table, and history chart. Practical guides cover P2P, remittances, and saving in dollars when the gap matters.

Why I Built This

Bolivians check the parallel dollar every day across WhatsApp groups and scattered sites. I wanted one clear Spanish tool with live rates, a converter, and practical USDT/P2P guides—without noise.
