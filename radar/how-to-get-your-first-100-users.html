How to Get Your First 100 Users After Launch

Launch day gives you a spike. Here's how to turn it into your first hundred real users, and keep the momentum after the feed moves on.

By The Runway· August 3, 2026· 2 min read

Launch day gives you a spike of attention. The founders who grow are the ones who turn that spike into a base, then keep the base growing after the feed forgets about them. Here's how to get from launch to your first hundred real users.

Convert the spike you get

Traffic you don't convert is traffic you wasted. Before you chase more attention, make sure the attention you're already getting turns into sign-ups: a clear value proposition, one obvious call to action, and the fastest possible path from "curious" to "using it." If your landing page isn't ready, fix that first with the pre-launch checklist.

Onboard your first users personally

At this stage, do things that don't scale. Personally welcome the people who sign up. Send a short note. Ask one honest question: what were you hoping this would do for you? Early users who feel seen become the people who refer you, and their answers are worth more than any dashboard.

Your first hundred users aren't a growth-hacking problem. They're a conversation. Have a hundred small ones and you'll learn exactly what to build and say next.

Keep showing up after day one

The launch-day feed moves on within hours; you shouldn't. Momentum comes from repetition:

  • Keep participating in the communities where your users gather.
  • Maintain permanent listings that keep sending discovery traffic for months.
  • Share progress in public, updates, milestones, lessons, so people can follow along.
  • Follow up with everyone who showed interest but didn't convert.

Turn users into a loop

Once you have a few dozen happy users, make it easy for them to bring the next few dozen. A simple ask ("know someone who'd find this useful?"), a shareable listing link, and a genuinely good experience do more than any referral gimmick. Most early referral revenue comes from lots of ordinary users each telling a few people, not from one big influencer.

Frequently asked

How do I get my first 100 users?
Combine a launch-day spike with steady follow-through: convert launch traffic with a clear CTA, personally onboard early users, ask them what's missing, and keep showing up in the communities where your users gather. The first hundred come from attention plus persistence, not one viral moment.
What should I do the week after launching?
Talk to everyone who signed up. Personal onboarding, a short thank-you, and one honest question about their experience will teach you more than any analytics dashboard, and turn early users into advocates.
How do I keep momentum after the launch spike?
Keep listing and sharing beyond day one: permanent directories, ongoing community participation, and consistent build-in-public updates all keep discovery flowing after the feed moves on.
