The Pre-Launch Website Checklist: 15 Things to Fix Before You List

Before you submit anywhere, spend an hour on these. They're the difference between a listing that converts and one people bounce from.

By Julie James, founder of LaunchFree.io· Last updated September 2, 2026· 3 min read

Before you submit anywhere, fix the essentials: a clear above-the-fold headline and CTA, fast mobile load, and analytics installed. Then work through the SEO and trust layers, unique meta tags, a social share image, a privacy page, a custom 404, so a live product with a clear CTA beats a perfect page that never ships.

What are the essentials to fix first?

ItemWhy it matters
Clear headline above the foldSays what it is and who it's for in one line
One obvious call to actionHard to miss, one primary action
Fast loadA slow page loses people before they read a word
Mobile layoutMost launch traffic is mobile
Analytics installedYou can't improve what you can't see

What's in the SEO and sharing layer?

  • Unique page titles and meta descriptions on every page.
  • A social share image (Open Graph) so shared links look credible.
  • Alt text on meaningful images.
  • A sitemap and robots.txt so search engines can crawl you.
  • Descriptive, readable URLs.

What's in the trust and polish layer?

  • A privacy page (and terms, if you collect data or payments).
  • A custom 404 page so broken links don't dump people on a dead end.
  • A thank-you or confirmation state after sign-up, so people know it worked.
  • A visible way to contact you. Even just an email builds trust.
  • A response-time promise if you offer support ("we reply within a day").

Don't let the list paralyze you. Nail the five essentials, launch, then work down the rest. A live product with a clear CTA beats a perfect page that never ships.

Frequently asked

What should I fix on my site before launching?
Prioritize the things visitors and search engines notice first: a clear headline and call-to-action above the fold, fast load time, mobile layout, unique page titles and meta descriptions, a social share image, analytics, and basics like a privacy page and custom 404.
Do I need all of this before I can list?
No. The must-haves are a clear value proposition, a working site, a call to action, and analytics. The rest strengthen the result. Don't let a long checklist stop you from launching; fix the essentials, then improve.
Why does a social share image matter?
Because when someone shares your link, the preview image is often the first thing people see. A clean Open Graph image makes your launch look credible in feeds and chats, and its absence makes it look broken.
What's the minimum viable pre-launch checklist?
A clear above-the-fold headline and call to action, a page that loads fast on mobile, and analytics installed. Those three cover most of the risk; everything else is worth doing but won't sink a launch if it's missing on day one.
How long does it take to work through this checklist?
Usually about an hour for a site that already exists. Most items are quick fixes, like writing a meta description or adding an Open Graph image, not rebuilds.
Why does a custom 404 page matter before launch?
Launch traffic clicks a lot of links fast, and some will be typed wrong or shared with a typo. A custom 404 keeps those visitors oriented instead of losing them to a dead, unbranded error page.
