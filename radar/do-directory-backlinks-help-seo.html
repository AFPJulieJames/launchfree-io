Do Directory Backlinks Still Help SEO in 2026?

Short answer: yes, but only the right ones. Here's how directory backlinks actually move the needle, and how to tell a valuable listing from a waste of time.

By The Runway· August 10, 2026· 2 min read

If you've spent an hour submitting your startup to directories, a fair question follows: did any of that actually help? The internet is full of both hype ("submit to 500 directories!") and dismissiveness ("directories are dead"). The truth sits in between, and it's simple once you know what to look for.

The short answer

Yes, directory backlinks still help SEO in 2026, but only the good ones. A single dofollow link from a trusted, relevant directory does more for you than fifty nofollow listings on abandoned sites. The lazy version of this tactic is dead. The selective version still works.

What makes a directory backlink actually valuable

Three things separate a listing worth having from noise:

  • Dofollow. If the link is nofollow, it passes no link equity. It can still drive discovery and referral clicks, but it won't move your rankings. Know which you're getting.
  • Relevance. A launch or startup directory linking to your startup is a natural, topical fit. A generic link farm is not, and search engines can tell.
  • Trust. Directories that review submissions and keep quality high are the ones search engines respect. Anywhere that lists anything instantly, with no review, tends to be worth less.

Rule of thumb: one relevant dofollow link from a curated, human-reviewed directory beats a spreadsheet of 200 auto-approved links every time.

Set expectations: this is foundational, not magic

Directory backlinks are best understood as foundational SEO. They help a young site establish early authority and pick up referral traffic while your content, product, and other signals mature. What they are not is an overnight rankings hack. If a service promises instant page-one results from directory links, that's your cue to walk away.

Frequently asked

Do directory backlinks still work for SEO in 2026?
Yes, when they're high quality. A dofollow link from a trusted, relevant directory passes real authority and helps your rankings. Low-quality links from spammy or dead directories do little or nothing, so quality and relevance matter far more than quantity.
What's the difference between a dofollow and nofollow backlink?
A dofollow link passes SEO authority (link equity) from the linking site to yours, which can help your search rankings. A nofollow link tells search engines not to pass that authority; it still brings discovery and referral traffic but no direct ranking benefit.
How many directories should I submit to?
Treat it as an ongoing habit rather than a one-time blast: a handful of relevant, quality directories per week is plenty. A few strong dofollow listings beat dozens of low-quality ones.
Are directory backlinks a fast SEO win?
No. Think of them as foundational SEO, not a growth hack. They help build early authority and referral traffic while your content and other signals mature; results compound over months, not days.
