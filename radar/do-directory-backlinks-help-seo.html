Do Directory Backlinks Still Help SEO in 2026?

Short answer: yes, but only the right ones. Here's how directory backlinks actually move the needle, and how to tell a valuable listing from a waste of time.

By Julie James, founder of LaunchFree.io· Last updated September 2, 2026· 3 min read

Yes, directory backlinks still help SEO in 2026, but only the good ones. A single dofollow link from a trusted, relevant, human-reviewed directory does more for your rankings than fifty nofollow listings on abandoned sites. The spray-and-pray version of this tactic is dead. The selective version still works.

What makes a directory backlink actually valuable?

Three things separate a listing worth having from noise: whether the link is dofollow, whether the directory is relevant to what you're launching, and whether the directory is trusted enough that search engines respect it. Get those three right and a single listing can outperform dozens of weak ones.

Rule of thumb: one relevant dofollow link from a curated, human-reviewed directory beats a spreadsheet of 200 auto-approved links every time.

Is a directory backlink a fast SEO win?

No, and treating it like one is where people get disappointed. Directory backlinks are foundational SEO: they help a young site establish early authority and pick up referral traffic while your content, product, and other signals mature. They are not an overnight rankings hack. If a service promises instant page-one results from directory links, that's the cue to walk away.

Frequently asked

Do directory backlinks still work for SEO in 2026?
Yes, when they're high quality. A dofollow link from a trusted, relevant directory passes real authority and helps your rankings. Low-quality links from spammy or dead directories do little or nothing, so quality and relevance matter far more than quantity.
What's the difference between a dofollow and nofollow backlink?
A dofollow link passes SEO authority (link equity) from the linking site to yours, which can help your search rankings. A nofollow link tells search engines not to pass that authority; it still brings discovery and referral traffic but no direct ranking benefit.
How many directories should I submit to?
Treat it as an ongoing habit rather than a one-time blast: a handful of relevant, quality directories per week is plenty. A few strong dofollow listings beat dozens of low-quality ones.
Are directory backlinks a fast SEO win?
No. Think of them as foundational SEO, not a growth hack. They help build early authority and referral traffic while your content and other signals mature; results compound over months, not days.
How do I check if a directory link is dofollow or nofollow?
View the page source and search for your link's anchor tag. If it has rel="nofollow", it's nofollow. No rel attribute, or a rel value without nofollow, means it's passing link equity as dofollow.
