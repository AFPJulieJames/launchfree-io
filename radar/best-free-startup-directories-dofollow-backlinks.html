The Best Free Startup Directories for Dofollow Backlinks (2026)

A shortlist of free directories that actually pass SEO value, plus how to submit efficiently without turning it into a second job.

By The Runway· August 6, 2026· 2 min read

There are aggregator pages out there listing 500, 900, even 1,000+ startup directories. Impressive, and mostly a trap. You do not need a thousand links; you need a dozen good ones. This is a shorter, honest shortlist focused on free directories that pass real SEO value, plus a way to submit without losing a weekend.

What "good" looks like

Before the list, the filter. A directory is worth your time if it's dofollow (passes link equity), relevant (startups/software, not a generic catch-all), and reviewed (someone checks submissions, which keeps quality and trust high). If it auto-approves anything instantly and marks links nofollow, skip it. We explain the mechanics in do directory backlinks still help SEO.

The shortlist

  • The Runway (LaunchFree.io) — free forever, human reviewed, permanent listing, and a dofollow backlink on every listing.
  • SaaSHub — software discovery with durable listings and comparison pages.
  • AlternativeTo — massive, evergreen, strong for long-tail discovery.
  • Launching Next — simple, permanent startup listing.
  • Indie Hackers products — a product page plus a community that pays attention.

Quality over quantity isn't a platitude here; it's the whole game. One dofollow link from a trusted, relevant directory beats fifty nofollow listings on dead sites.

How to submit without it becoming a chore

Turn it into a small, repeatable habit instead of a marathon:

  1. Write your listing copy once, name, one-line tagline, a short description, and reuse it everywhere.
  2. Submit to a few quality directories per week, not all at once.
  3. Keep a simple list of where you've submitted and which links are dofollow.
  4. Prioritize reviewed directories first; their links carry more trust.

The bottom line

Ignore the 1,000-directory lists. Pick a dozen relevant, dofollow, reviewed directories, submit at a sustainable pace, and let the links age. Start with one that gives you a dofollow link by default: list free on The Runway.

Frequently asked

Which free directories give dofollow backlinks?
Reviewed, quality-focused directories are your best bet. The Runway offers a free dofollow backlink on every listing; broader discovery sites like SaaSHub and AlternativeTo also provide lasting listings. Always verify the link type, since some directories mark links nofollow.
How do I check if a directory link is dofollow?
Once listed, inspect your link in the page source and look for rel="nofollow" on the anchor tag. If it's absent, the link is dofollow. Browser SEO extensions can highlight nofollow links automatically.
Is it worth submitting to lots of directories?
Only quality ones, and only at a sustainable pace. A handful of relevant, reviewed, dofollow directories does more than a mass blast to low-quality sites. Spread submissions over weeks rather than doing them all at once.
