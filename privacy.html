Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026  ·  Automated Income Tools LLC  ·  hello@launchfree.io

The short version: We collect only what we need to run LaunchFree.io. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with anyone except the services required to operate the site. You can contact us anytime to have your data removed.

Who we are

LaunchFree.io ("The Runway") is a free product and launch directory operated by Automated Income Tools LLC, based in Phoenix, Arizona. You can reach us at hello@launchfree.io.

What information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

Information you provide directly: when you submit a listing via our submission form, we collect:

  • Launch or product name and URL
  • Tagline, description, and category
  • Your name and email address
  • Optional: Twitter/X handle, LinkedIn URL, logo URL
  • Whether you opted in to our newsletter

Information collected automatically: when you visit LaunchFree.io, we collect standard analytics data including:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Browser type and device type
  • Referring URL
  • General geographic location (country/city level)

How we use your information

  • To review and publish your launch listing on LaunchFree.io
  • To contact you about your submission status
  • To send you The Runway Radar newsletter (if you opted in)
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve it

Third-party services we use

We use the following third-party services to operate LaunchFree.io:

  • Airtable: stores submission data. Privacy policy
  • Make.com (formerly Integromat): processes form submissions and routes data to Airtable. Privacy policy
  • Beehiiv: powers The Runway Radar newsletter. Only subscribers who opted in are added. Privacy policy
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): anonymous site analytics. Privacy policy
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website. Privacy policy

Cookies

LaunchFree.io uses minimal cookies. Google Analytics places cookies to track anonymous usage. We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Data retention

Submission data is retained in Airtable for as long as your listing is active on LaunchFree.io. If you request removal of your listing and data, we will delete it within 30 days. Newsletter subscriber data is managed by Beehiiv and you can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of our newsletter at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@launchfree.io.

Children's privacy

LaunchFree.io is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of LaunchFree.io after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at hello@launchfree.io.
