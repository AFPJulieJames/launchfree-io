Questions about the runway

Everything you might want to know before you list, from how the free backlink works to what gets accepted. Still stuck? Email hello@launchfree.io and we'll help.

Is LaunchFree.io really free?

Yes. Listing on LaunchFree.io is free forever. There's no listing fee, no approval fee, and no credit card required. The core listing will always be free. We cover costs through optional sponsored placements, never by charging builders to be discovered.

What do I get when I list my launch?

Every approved launch gets a permanent listing page at launchfree.io/listings/your-launch, a free dofollow backlink to your site, a "Listed on The Runway" badge you can add to your own site, community upvotes, and a shot at being featured in the weekly Runway Radar newsletter.

Do I get a dofollow backlink?

Yes. Every listing includes a permanent dofollow backlink to your site. It passes real SEO value and helps your domain authority from day one, at no cost.

How long does it take for my launch to go live?

We review every submission by hand, usually within 24 hours. We check that your site is live, real, and matches its description before it goes on the runway. Once approved, you get an email with a link to your listing page.

What kinds of launches are accepted?

Any legal business, product, service, or idea. Software, SaaS, developer tools, newsletters, creator tools, side projects, and more are all welcome, at every stage and in every niche.

We don't accept illegal products, adult content, gambling, weapons, scams, pyramid schemes, spam tools, hateful content, or duplicate and fake listings.

Can I list a pre-launch, beta, or waitlist product?

Yes. Pre-launch, beta, waitlist, early access, MVPs, and works in progress all belong on the runway. If you're building it, it belongs here, no matter the stage.

How do I get featured in the Runway Radar newsletter?

Every Friday, the top launches of the week go out to our subscribers in the Runway Radar. Listing is free and every launch is eligible. Community upvotes help top-voted listings rise into the weekly highlights.

Can I add my logo to my listing later?

Yes. New listings start with a clean letter mark. If you'd like your real logo on your page, just reply to your approval email with a link to your logo anytime and we'll add it.

Can I edit or remove my listing?

Yes. Email hello@launchfree.io with your listing and the change you need, whether that's an edit to your details or removing the listing entirely, and we'll take care of it.

How is LaunchFree.io different from Product Hunt?

LaunchFree.io gives every launch a permanent home with a dofollow backlink, rather than a single launch day that scrolls away. There's no premium upsell, no pay-to-play placement, and no approval fee. Every listing is human reviewed and free forever.
