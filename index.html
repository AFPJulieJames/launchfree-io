Why this exists

Born on a
frustrating Tuesday.

On May 6th, 2026, we were trying to get our launch in front of people. We hit a paywall on one platform. A months-long approval queue on another. A premium listing upsell on a third.

Every platform that was supposed to help people launch had found a way to charge them for the privilege. We thought that was wrong.

So we built LaunchFree.io. The place where anyone building anything gets a fair shot at being discovered, regardless of budget, background, or business model.

What you get when you list

Concrete. Honest. Free.

No vague promises. Here's exactly what listing on LaunchFree.io does for your launch.

01
A permanent listing
Your launch lives on LaunchFree.io permanently, indexed by search engines and discoverable by anyone searching for what you're building.
02
Weekly newsletter feature
Every Friday, new listings are featured in The Runway Radar, sent to builders and makers actively looking for new launches to try and share.
03
Community upvotes
Visitors can upvote your launch. Top-voted listings rise to the top of the feed and earn a spot in weekly radar highlights.

06
Zero fees. Ever.
The core listing is free. It will always be free. We make money through optional sponsored placements, never by charging builders to be discovered.
The Runway Standard

Who belongs on the runway

If you're building it, it belongs here. Every stage, every budget, every niche. One standard: be real.

Free doesn't mean anything goes. The Runway has one job: to be the most trusted free launch directory on the internet. Every listing is real, every founder is legitimate, every launch serves a genuine purpose. We review every submission before it goes live.

✦ Welcome on the runway

  • ✓Any legal business, product, service, or idea
  • ✓Pre-launch, beta, waitlist, and early access
  • ✓Side projects, MVPs, and works in progress
  • ✓Solo builders, small teams, first-time founders
  • ✓Every industry, niche, and category
  • ✓Free and paid launches both welcome
  • ✓International founders and global launches

✗ Not on this runway

  • ✗Illegal products, services, or businesses
  • ✗Anything that promotes harm to people or animals
  • ✗Adult content, gambling, or weapons
  • ✗Scams, pyramid schemes, or deceptive launches
  • ✗Spam tools or products designed to manipulate
  • ✗Hate-based or discriminatory content
  • ✗Duplicate or fake listings
