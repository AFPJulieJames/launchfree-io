Why this exists

Born on a
frustrating Tuesday.

On May 6th, 2026, we were trying to launch a product. We hit a paywall on one platform. A months-long approval queue on another. A "premium listing" upsell on a third.

Every platform that was supposed to help bootstrap founders launch had found a way to charge them for the privilege. We thought that was wrong.

So we built LaunchFree.io — The Runway. The place where every legal, legitimate product gets a fair shot at being discovered, regardless of the founder's budget.

The Runway Standard

Who belongs on the runway

The Runway is open to every legal, legitimate product — from zero revenue to a million. We welcome every stage, every budget, every niche. One standard: be real.

Free doesn't mean anything goes. The Runway has one job: to be the most trusted free launch directory on the internet. That means every listing is real, every founder is legitimate, and every product serves a genuine purpose. We review every submission before it goes live.

✦ Welcome on the runway

  • ✓Any legal product, tool, or service at any stage
  • ✓Pre-launch, beta, waitlist, and early access products
  • ✓Side projects, MVPs, and works in progress
  • ✓Bootstrap founders, solo makers, and small teams
  • ✓Every industry, niche, and category
  • ✓Free and paid products both welcome
  • ✓International founders and global products

✗ Not on this runway

  • ✗Illegal products, services, or businesses
  • ✗Products that promote harm to people or animals
  • ✗Adult content, gambling, or weapons
  • ✗Scams, pyramid schemes, or deceptive products
  • ✗Spam tools or products designed to manipulate
  • ✗Hate-based or discriminatory products
  • ✗Duplicate or fake listings

See something that shouldn't be here? Report it to us — we review every flag within 24 hours and remove listings that violate The Runway Standard. Our goal is a directory every founder trusts.
