Questions

Good to know

What is the shortest placement you offer?

Homepage features are booked by the week. Longer runs and bundles with the Runway Radar are where sponsors usually see the most value, and I am happy to put those together.

Can I pick my dates?

Yes, based on availability. Because there is only one sponsor at a time, earlier requests get first pick.

Do you guarantee clicks or signups?

No one honestly can. What you get is a prominent, clearly labeled spot in front of an audience of active builders, plus the numbers so you can judge the results for yourself.

Will sponsoring help my own listing?

No. Your listing is free and is treated exactly like every other listing. Sponsorship is kept completely separate from reviews, rankings, and votes.

What kinds of products are a fit?

Tools and services that help people build, launch, and grow: developer tools, AI platforms, hosting, payments, analytics, design, and marketing. If you are unsure, just ask.
